Aarohan Merit Scholarship 2026-27: Official Notice

Published: 12 August 2026

Eligibility: Applicants must have a family income below ₹4,00,000 per year and at least 75% marks in Class 12.

The scholarship amount is ₹50,000 per year.

Applicants must submit an income certificate, last-year marksheet and a photo ID.

Last date to apply: 30 November 2026.

Apply online at the application portal: https://apply.foundation.example/aarohan
